## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `yc-parody` meets `polished`
- Creative direction: Silicon Valley launch film meets brutal developer reality check
- Interpretation: Ultra-crisp light studio layout with subtle ambient elevation shadows (`0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0,0,0,0.07)`), vibrant orange flame accents, and fluid 3D CSS transforms.
- Angle: Recreate the exact shot pacing and spatial energy of reference_vide4.mp4, replacing marketing agency footage with the hilarious, relatable truth of getting your GitHub dissected.
- Hook: A pristine smartphone conversation that turns into an explosive 3D pull-back with orbiting developer cards.
- Outro / punchline: "GitRoasted. Brutal feedback. Better developers."

## Storyboard
1. Scene 1 (0.0s - 4.5s) — Mobile Hook & 3D Tilt: Phone standing center, 2 iMessage bubbles, 3D tilt back with 5 floating reaction cards bursting out.
2. Scene 2 (4.5s - 8.5s) — Reality Check Callouts: Kinetic 3D cards landing on beat with brutal GitHub diagnostics.
3. Scene 3 (8.5s - 12.5s) — 3D Layered Dashboard: Isometric perspective sweep over stacked commit charts, language distribution, and profile telemetry.
4. Scene 4 (12.5s - 16.5s) — Seriousness Score Turntable: 3D circular dial counter spinning up to 464/1000 with flame progress ring and verdict badge.
5. Scene 5 (16.5s - 19.5s) — Interactive Quick Wins: Simulated cursor click resolving CI/CD and repo issues, leaping score to 599 (+135 pts).
6. Scene 6 (19.5s - 22.0s) — Definitive Brand Lockup: GitRoasted flame emblem, clean typography, tagline, and URL.
