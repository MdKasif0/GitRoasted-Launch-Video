# Brag Plan: GitRoasted

## What is this app?
GitRoasted is an AI-powered developer roast engine and profile auditor that scans your GitHub commits, abandoned repos, and streak gaps to deliver a brutal Seriousness Score (464/1000) and an actionable Quick Wins rehabilitation roadmap.

## The angle
Your GitHub profile is saying more about you than you intended (and right now it's giving unemployable). We took the high-production studio aesthetic of premier tech launch films — 3D camera moves, pristine light layouts, physical depth, and fluid motion design — and applied it with razor-sharp comedic precision to the developer experience of getting publicly roasted.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A photorealistic 3D smartphone resting in a pristine light studio. An incoming iMessage pops:
*"Hey... can someone check my GitHub?"* followed immediately by the reply: *"Let us roast real quick 🔥"* with a live camera preview of the roast squad.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **3D Mobile Pullback & Floating Reaction Cards (2.5s - 4.5s)**: The phone tilts back in 3D perspective as floating rounded reaction clips burst into the 3D space around it.
- **Developer Reality Check (4.5s - 8.5s)**: Rapid-fire brutal diagnostics: "47 Abandoned Repos", "Commit: 'fixed bug final v2'", "8-Month Streak Gap".
- **Layered 3D Dashboard Glide (8.5s - 12.5s)**: Smooth 3D perspective glide over stacked cards showing commit telemetry, code smell scans, and profile breakdown.
- **The Seriousness Score Turntable (12.5s - 16.5s)**: A 3D turntable dial counting from 000 up to **464 / 1000** ("Rising Developer"), complete with circular progress gauge and roast verdict card.
- **Interactive Quick Wins (+135 pts) (16.5s - 19.5s)**: Interactive checklist simulation where resolving issues pumps the score from 464 → 599 ("WE ARE SO BACK 🚀").

## Outro / punchline
"GitRoasted. Brutal feedback. Better developers." Clean, definitive brand lockup with flame icon on pure studio white, followed by "github.com/roast".

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Dev shares profile / username with confidence.
2. **Roast Result**: AI dissects commit history and assigns 464 / 1000 Seriousness Score.
3. **Rehabilitation**: Interactive Quick Wins boosts score by +135 points.
